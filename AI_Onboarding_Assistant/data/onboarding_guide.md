# Onboarding Guide

## Laptop Setup

New employees receive a company laptop through the IT onboarding process.

On the first day, employees should:
1. Sign in using the temporary credentials provided by IT.
2. Change the temporary password.
3. Enroll the company device in the required device-management service.
4. Complete the security awareness training.

## Account Activation

Employees should contact the onboarding coordinator through the internal support channel for account activation problems.

The onboarding coordinator can help with:
- Laptop setup
- Account activation
- First-day orientation

## VPN Access

VPN access is requested through the approved access-request process described in the Security Policy.

Employees must not bypass access controls or share credentials.
