# Product FAQ

## Onboarding Portal

The onboarding portal provides links to approved onboarding resources and company product documentation.

Employees can use the portal to find:
- Onboarding guides
- Product documentation
- Approved internal resources

## Product Documentation

Product documentation questions should be answered only when the relevant information appears in the approved product documentation.

If the requested information is not present in the approved documentation, the assistant must not invent an answer.

## Unsupported Topics

The onboarding assistant does not provide:
- Stock prices
- Personal financial advice
- External news
- Payroll information
- Private employee information

Questions about these topics are outside the approved knowledge base for this assistant.

## Getting Help

For problems with the onboarding portal, employees should contact the internal support channel.

Employees should never provide passwords, authentication tokens, API keys, or other secrets when requesting support.
